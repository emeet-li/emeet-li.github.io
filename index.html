Eli Broad College of Business Michigan State University Advisor: Dr. Brian T. Pentland

I am a PhD candidate in Information Technology Management at Eli Broad College of Business, Michigan State University. In my research, I use digital trace to understsand organizational dynamics in varied contexts including healthcare and open source software development. I am enthusiastic about viewing the world as relational, so I am interested in relational patterns (often represented as networks) and how those relational patterns change over time.

Dissertation

Brief overview of my dissertation.

Routines as Recurrent Patterns in Complex Dynamical Systems

This work aims to enable scholars to view organizations as complex dynamical systems with a computational method - Temporal Mapper. The method represents a first-time transfer from neuroscience to organizational studies.

Transitions between Recurrent Patterns of Organizing: A Path View

With the debut of GenAI, open source communities could revamp the ways of organizing in software development. This work aims to understand the endogenous and exogenous factors that influence the trajectories of open source software development.

Emergence of Recurrence in Collaborative Organizing

This work aims to unpack why recurrent patterns of organizing emerge and dissolve in the context of open source software development.
